# NextWave Crew Product Blueprint

## Positioning
NextWave Crew is a global membership platform for people who work at sea. It combines professional identity, community, career mobility and contract-aware financial wellbeing in one account.

Tagline: **Work at sea. Build life on shore.**

## Primary users
Passenger ship, cruise, cargo, tanker, offshore, ferry and yacht workers across casino, hotel, deck, engine, culinary, entertainment, retail, spa, medical, security, HR and shore-side roles.

## Product pillars
1. **CrewID**: portable professional identity, sea service, credentials and verification.
2. **Community**: private professional spaces organized by department, vessel type, country and career stage.
3. **Career Center**: maritime CV, document reminders, job matching and application tracking.
4. **CrewWealth**: contract-income planning, shore reserve, emergency goals, retirement scenarios and investment education.
5. **Membership**: Free, Plus and Pro member plans plus B2B recruiter/company plans.
6. **SeaPoints**: non-transferable member rewards for verified useful activity. No market price, trading or promised return.

## First public release
The MVP must be valuable without financial custody or a tradable crypto asset. Public launch gates are auth, privacy controls, community moderation, CrewID, job/recruiter verification, CrewWealth education boundaries, accessibility, mobile performance, backups and audit logs.
